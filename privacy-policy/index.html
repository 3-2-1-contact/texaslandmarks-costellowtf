TexasLandmarks, Texas Historical Markers Map

Over 13,000 official Texas Historical Commission markers. Full text, offline.

Free on the App Store. No ads, no tracking.

Why you'll want it

  • Works anywhere, even with no signal

    Everything runs off a database built into the app. No signal required, no loading spinner on a backroad with no bars.

  • See what's coming up before you pass it

    The app shows your distance and direction to nearby markers, with an "ahead of you" filter so you can see what's coming up before you get there.

  • Apple Maps gets you there

    Tap through to Apple Maps for turn-by-turn directions when you do want to stop.

100% Free, No Catch

TexasLandmarks is free. Not a free trial, not a freemium tier with a paywall behind it. Just free.

You download it, it works, that's it.

Privacy Policy

TexasLandmarks collects no data. It's fully offline. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

Location stays on your phone. If you allow location access, your position is used on the device to find nearby markers. It's never uploaded.

No third parties receive any data, because none is collected.

Nothing is retained, so there's nothing to delete and no account to cancel.

No accounts, no sign-in. The app just works.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads. This page doesn't track you either.

Built by one developer who got tired of driving past history without knowing what it was.
